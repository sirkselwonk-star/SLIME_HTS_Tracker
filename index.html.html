Flow map

Each bubble is an account, sized by balance. Dots travel from sender to receiver for the period shown. Green bubbles grew that period, pink ones shrank. A dark ring marks a contract, a lime ring a likely pool. Tap a bubble to isolate its inflows and outflows.

Wallets
Balances come from the public Hedera mirror node. Each snapshot is the balance record closest to, and not after, the snapshot date. Tap a wallet to see its balance at every snapshot and pull its individual transfers. Contracts are confirmed against the mirror node. "Likely pool" is a guess: a contract that holds this token alongside one or two other fungible tokens. Labels you add are saved in this browser.
